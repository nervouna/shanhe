A turn-based 4X strategy game for the browser, played on the real terrain of East Asia, Siberia and Southeast Asia. Fifty fictional nations follow real province-level borders and take their flavour from the real places. Units are xiangqi-style pieces on a sand-table map.

## Status

- **Maturity**: design complete, no playable build yet. Development is tracked in the [issues](https://github.com/nervouna/shanhe/issues).
- **Who it is for**: people curious about what a language model can build on its own. The whole game, from design to code, is written by Claude (Anthropic's Fable model) with a human owner who makes product decisions.
- **What it will take to run**: a desktop or tablet browser with WebGPU; WebGL is the fallback. Nothing to install.
- **Languages**: Chinese and English UI.
- **Releases**: none yet.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What makes it different

- **Everything visual is generated by code.** No model files and no texture files. Terrain comes from public elevation and border datasets; materials come from shaders.
- **Provinces stay meaningful.** Borders are fixed at the start. A nation develops freely inside its province and grows beyond it only through war or diplomacy.
- **A full game in about two hours**: 150 turns across six eras, from ancient to modern.
- **Three ways to win**: conquest, science, economy.

## License

[MIT](LICENSE).

The map in `public/data/` is derived from public geographic datasets, which keep their own licences. They are described in [tools/pipeline/DATA.md](tools/pipeline/DATA.md):

- Borders, coastline, lakes and rivers: made with [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public domain).
- Elevation and sea floor: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, 2022: ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model, https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74 (CC0 1.0).
- Climate, from which terrain and vegetation are derived: Beck, H.E., N.E. Zimmermann, T.R. McVicar, N. Vergopolan, A. Berg, E.F. Wood: Present and future Köppen-Geiger climate classification maps at 1-km resolution, Nature Scientific Data, 2018, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.6396959, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The climate classes were sampled per hex and turned into the game's terrain classes.
